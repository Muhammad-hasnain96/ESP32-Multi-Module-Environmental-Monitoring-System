4. PCB Layout & Routing Engineering Guidelines

⚡ Trace Geometries & Power Distribution

  • +5V0 & +3V3 Power Traces: 24 to 32 mils (0.6mm – 0.8mm) to minimize I²R drop.
  • Analog Signals (GPIO 1, GPIO 2): 10 to 12 mils. Keep strictly away from relay coils, switching DC-DC inductors, and crystal oscillators.
  • Digital Signals (I2C, DHT, Flow): 10 to 12 mils with 3W spacing to prevent crosstalk.

🛡️ Ground Plane & Decoupling Strategy

  • Solid Ground Pour: Maintain an unbroken GND copper plane on Layer 2 (Bottom layer).
  • Decoupling Capacitors: Place 100nF ceramic caps within < 3mm of sensor connector pins and ESP32 VDD pins.
  • Star Grounding: Connect analog sensor grounds (TDS & pH) directly to main system GND to prevent return current offsets.
