⚡ 1. Trace Widths

• 5V Power Net: 30 mil (0.75 mm) to handle MQ-137 heater current (160mA).
• 3.3V Power Net: 20 mil (0.5 mm).
• Signals / I2C: 10-12 mil (0.25-0.3 mm).

🔋 2. Decoupling Capacitors

• Bulk Capacitor: 100µF 16V electrolytic capacitor directly across 5V DC input.
• Local Caps: 0.1µF (100nF) ceramic capacitors right next to CCS811, MQ-137, and LCD headers.

🛡️ 3. Safe Flashing Isolation

• GPIO 19 & 20: Completely unrouted! Native USB D- and D+ are preserved.
• You can plug in the USB cable to flash code or monitor Serial anytime without unplugging sensors.
